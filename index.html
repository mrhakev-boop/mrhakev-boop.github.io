About

I'm Kevin, a photographer who works light, handheld and close to the action. I follow the day instead of staging it, and when a moment calls for a proper portrait, I slow down and direct it. I'm currently working out of New Jersey and moving to Seattle, and I'm happy to travel for the right day. Expect me nearby but not in the way, with photos that look the way the day felt.
